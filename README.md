# Free English and reading resources for kids in Australia

[Airbotix cheatsheets: read and share](CHEATSHEETS.md)

[English](https://github.com/Airbotix-AI/free-english-reading-resources-for-kids-australia) · [简体中文](https://github.com/Airbotix-AI/free-english-reading-resources-for-kids-australia-zh) · [한국어](https://github.com/Airbotix-AI/free-english-reading-resources-for-kids-australia-ko)

A Markdown guide for Australian families: 18 resources for English language, early literacy and reading, with 14 age/year starting points from preschool to Year 12. Maintained by [Airbotix](https://airbotix.ai/). Guide language does not change the language offered by external resources.

Corresponding article: [Reading websites for Australian children](https://airbotix.ai/blog/best-reading-websites-for-kids-australia)

Access evidence reviewed: 19 October 2026. No child accounts, paid trials or member-only sessions were opened. Sources describe access conditions; they do not prove every activity works on every device.

| Resource | Suggested age / AU stage | Purpose and access boundary | Official evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Literacy Hub](https://www.literacyhub.edu.au/for-families/) | 3–8 / Preschool–Year 2 | Family literacy guidance and shared texts; adult selects the text and follows the school’s phonics sequence. Public pages are separate from the registered Year 1 Phonics Check. | [↗](https://www.literacyhub.edu.au/plan-teach-and-assess/shared-reading-texts/) |
| [ABC Education: Learn English](https://www.abc.net.au/education/learn-english) | 10–18 / Years 5–12 | Free English-language lessons and articles, including EAL practice. Adult selects age-appropriate topics; business and university material is not automatically suitable for children. Separate iview playback may have different conditions. | [↗](https://help.abc.net.au/hc/en-us/articles/360001140855-What-is-Learn-English) |
| [ABC BTN](https://www.abc.net.au/btn/classroom) | 10–16 / Years 5–10 | Public news stories and teacher downloads for summarising and checking evidence. Preview sensitive current affairs; read the available transcript as well as watching. Not a phonics course. | [↗](https://www.abc.net.au/btn/classroom) |
| [Oxford Owl for Home](https://www.oxfordowl.co.uk/for-home/library-page) | 3–11 / Preschool–Year 5 | Selected free eBooks; parent/carer registration required. Choose by taught sounds and reading needs. UK book bands are not Australian Years; the paid school catalogue is separate. | [↗](https://support.oxfordowl.co.uk/parent-support/ebook-libraries/ebook-library-user-guide/) |
| [Storyline Online](https://storylineonline.net/) | 5–10 / Foundation–Year 4 | Free picture-book read-aloud videos for discussion. Listening is not independent decoding. US grade labels need interpretation; link to original videos, never download or repost them. | [↗](https://storylineonline.net/comments-and-questions/) |
| [LearnEnglish Kids](https://learnenglishkids.britishcouncil.org/read-write/reading-practice) | 5–12 / Foundation–Year 6 | Free public reading practice, stories and language activities. Registration is relevant to commenting; paid courses are separate. Provider levels describe English ability, not Australian school placement. | [↗](https://learnenglishkids.britishcouncil.org/getting-started-for-kids) |
| [LearnEnglish Teens](https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/skills/reading) | 13–18 / Years 8–12 | Free public reading texts and exercises for teenage English learners. Choose CEFR level by ability; paid teaching offers are separate. No claim of HSC, VCE or other senior syllabus coverage. | [↗](https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/getting-started) |
| [Teach Your Monster to Read](https://www.teachyourmonster.org/teach-your-monster-to-read-overview/) | 4–7 / Preschool–Year 1 | Computer browser game is free; adult account and email confirmation are required. Mobile purchases/subscriptions are separate. Match UK sounds and sequence with classroom teaching. | [↗](https://www.teachyourmonster.org/teach-your-monster-to-read-overview/) |
| [StoryWeaver — Pratham Books](https://storyweaver.org.in/en/) | 4–12 / Preschool–Year 6 | Free multilingual stories; select English and a suitable reading level. Account-dependent creation tools are separate. Check each item’s licence, especially audio/video. Official indexed evidence checked; live page retrieval failed in this review. | [↗](https://storyweaver.org.in/en/about) |
| [UFLI Foundations Toolbox](https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/foundations/toolbox/) | 5–8 / Foundation–Year 2 | Free companion passages and practice resources for adult-led teaching. The teaching manual is sold separately; the Toolbox is not a complete free course. Check the Australian sequence and provider copyright rules. | [↗](https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/faq/) |
| [FCRR Student Center Activities](https://fcrr.org/student-center-activities) | 4–11 / Preschool–Year 5 | Public practice activities for skills already taught; adult preparation needed. US Pre-K–Grade 5 labels are not AU mapping. Non-profit educational use conditions apply; link rather than mirror or adapt the files. | [↗](https://fcrr.org/student-center-activities) |
| [Queensland libraries: LOTE4Kids](https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/collections/public-library-collections/collections-all-queensland-public-libraries) | 3–10 / Preschool–Year 4 | Conditional library-member access to multilingual books with English translations. Start from your Queensland public library login; ask about eligibility. This does not establish access for every Australian family or every library platform. | [↗](https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/collections/public-library-collections/collections-all-queensland-public-libraries) |
| [Unite for Literacy](https://www.uniteforliteracy.com/) | 3–8 / Preschool–Year 2 | Free picture books and narration without registration. Choose English text and check the narration languages for the selected title. Shared reading and vocabulary, not a sequenced phonics course. | [↗](https://www.uniteforliteracy.com/corp/faq) |
| [Reading Rockets: Reading Adventure Packs](https://www.readingrockets.org/literacy-home/literacy-tips-activities/reading-adventure-packs) | 3–9 / Preschool–Year 3 | Free printable activity instructions for adult-led reading. Suggested books must be borrowed or obtained separately. Official indexed description checked; live retrieval returned 403. Do not assume the packs include the books. | [↗](https://www.readingrockets.org/literacy-home/literacy-tips-activities/reading-adventure-packs) |
| [Storynory](https://www.storynory.com/) | 6–12 / Years 1–6 | Free website stories with audio and written text. Adult previews themes and length, then pairs listening with reading a selected passage. Donations and shop links are separate; no ad-free claim is made. | [↗](https://www.storynory.com/stella-and-the-surprise-delivery/) |
| [Oxford Owl: activity sheets](https://home.oxfordowl.co.uk/kids-activities/) | 3–11 / Preschool–Year 5 | Selected free printable English activities, including writing and comprehension. Separate from the parent-login eBook library. UK age/year and SATs labels are not Australian curriculum equivalence; linked books may cost money. | [↗](https://home.oxfordowl.co.uk/kids-activities/) |
| [Words for Life — National Literacy Trust](https://wordsforlife.org.uk/) | 3–12 / Preschool–Year 6 | Free family chat, reading and writing activity ideas, selected by age. UK events and book recommendations may need adaptation. Audible partnerships, book purchases and other paid offers are excluded from the free scope. | [↗](https://literacytrust.org.uk/communities/rotherham/for-families/) |
| [NSW Stage 5 reading strategies](https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/literacy-and-numeracy/teaching-and-learning-resources/literacy/teaching-strategies/stage-5/reading) | 14–16 / Years 9–10 | Public teacher resources on inference, bias, sources and text structure. Adult selects and teaches a suitable activity. Stage 5 is a NSW label; this is not a complete senior English course or an autonomous student app. | [↗](https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/literacy-and-numeracy/teaching-and-learning-resources/literacy/teaching-strategies/stage-5/reading) |
