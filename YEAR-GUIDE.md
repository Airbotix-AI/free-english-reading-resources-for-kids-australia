# Year-by-year guide

Age/year matching below is editorial guidance, not a certified curriculum map or entry-age rule. State cut-offs and readiness vary. Foundation may be called Prep, Reception, Kindergarten, Transition or Pre-primary. UK stages, US grades and CEFR levels do not convert directly into Australian Years. EAL learners should choose appropriate language complexity without losing age-appropriate themes.
